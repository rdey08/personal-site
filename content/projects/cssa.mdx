## Context

NMSU's computer science students had no organization carrying the community:
no regular programming practice, no standing bridge to industry, and no event
that brought the major together. CSSA was founded in August 2025 to be that
organization, and I served as its founding president through May 2026.

## Building the organization

The first year was mostly infrastructure. CSSA needed a reason for students to
show up more than once, so the calendar was built around three recurring
formats: programming contests for practice, industry panels for the career
question students actually had, and social events for the part that keeps
people coming back. The aim throughout was career readiness and belonging.

It also needed money that did not depend on any one officer. I secured **$5,000
per year in operational funding**, which is what turned the calendar from a
best-effort volunteer schedule into something the next set of officers could
inherit and run.

## hackNMSU

The largest thing CSSA did was **hackNMSU, the first hackathon in NMSU's
history**. It drew **55 participants across 18 teams**.

I led sponsorship, judge recruitment, and event operations. Sponsorship was the
binding constraint: a first-time event at a university with no hackathon track
record has no prior turnout to point at, so every conversation started from
nothing. Judge recruitment had the same shape, needing people willing to commit
a weekend to an event nobody had seen before.

The event was covered by the [NMSU
Newsroom](https://newsroom.nmsu.edu/news/nmsu-students-build--code-and-showcase-projects-at-inaugural-hack-nmsu/s/7bf856a4-f1e1-4831-a1ef-ced257bb6423)
and [Organ Mountain
News](https://www.organmountainnews.com/nmsu-hack-nmsu-student-projects-inaugural-event/).

## What this shows

Founding an organization is a different problem from running one. There was no
membership list, no budget line, no sponsor relationships, and no precedent for
a hackathon to point at, so most of the year went into building the things a
later president gets to assume. The funding line and hackNMSU are the two
pieces most likely to outlast my term, which is the part I would want judged.
