Our abstract, "The PDS Atmospheric Modeling Annex: Adapting to the Community
Need for a Planetary Atmospheres Modeling Output Repository" (Neakrase et al.),
was accepted as a poster presentation at the 2026 AGU Annual Meeting, coming to
San Francisco, CA this December. It covers the same Atmospheric Modeling Annex
work as the DPS 58 poster, with the same seven authors. The full citation lives
under [Publications](/research).
