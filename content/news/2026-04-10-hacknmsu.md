hackNMSU, NMSU's first hackathon, brought 55 students from majors across
campus, computer science to nursing to aerospace engineering, to Science Hall
for a seven-hour build day. As CSSA Founding President I led sponsorship,
judge recruitment, and event operations, backed by the NMSU Computer Science
Department, the NMSU FinTech Lab, and the Hunt Center for Entrepreneurship.
