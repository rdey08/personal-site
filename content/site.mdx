I work on applied machine learning for scientific problems: models that fuse
messy, multi-modal real-world data into predictions scientists can use. At the
NMSU KDD Lab, my senior-thesis research uses multi-branch LSTMs to predict net
primary productivity and biomass change across 1.3 million records of
rangeland data: satellite, precipitation, cattle-GPS, and land-cover signals. At NASA's Planetary Data
System (Atmospheres Node), I develop ELSA, the web application planetary
scientists use to generate and archive metadata for large datasets.
